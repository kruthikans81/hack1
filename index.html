STAGE 1: Pre-Election Preparation — Form 6 Registration & Electoral Roll Search.
STEP 1: VOTER REGISTRATION

Form 6 Online Registration

Verify eligibility (Indian Citizen, 18+ years of age) and submit Form 6 online via voters.eci.gov.in or Voter Helpline App by uploading valid photo identity, address proof, and a passport-size photo.

STEP 2: ELECTORAL ROLL SEARCH

Check Electoral Roll & Locate Polling Station

Search electoralsearch.eci.gov.in to confirm your name appears on the official voter list and note down your Constituency, Polling Station address, and Serial Number.

📌 Important Rules for Casting the Vote (EVM & VVPAT)

  • Prohibited Devices: Mobile phones, cameras, and electronic devices are strictly prohibited inside the private voting compartment.
  • Selecting Candidate: Press the BLUE button next to your preferred candidate's name and symbol on the Electronic Voting Machine (EVM).
  • Visual Verification (7-Second Window): Look at the transparent glass window of the attached VVPAT printer. A printed paper slip displaying the candidate's serial number, name, and symbol will remain visible for 7 seconds before dropping into the lower sealed box.
  • Audio Confirmation (2-Second Beep): Wait for the continuous 2-second long BEEP sound from the machine, confirming your vote has been recorded.

8 Stages of the Election Process

Click on any stage below to explore key procedures, statutory rules, and constitutional safeguards.

01

Election Notification

The official announcement specifying voting dates, constituency boundaries, and filing deadlines.

Key Procedures: Published in official gazette notifications. The Model Code of Conduct comes into immediate effect across all participating districts.
02

Nomination Filing

Candidates submit their formal nomination papers and financial/criminal affidavits to the Returning Officer.

Key Procedures: Candidates pay security deposits, submit sworn affidavits (Form 26), and declare assets, liabilities, and educational qualifications.
03

Scrutiny of Nominations

Returning Officers inspect nominations for legal validity and compliance with electoral qualifications.

Key Procedures: Opposing candidates can raise objections. Invalid or incomplete nominations are formally rejected after hearing.
04

Withdrawal & Symbol Allotment

Candidates may withdraw their nominations. Registered and independent candidates receive official election symbols.

Key Procedures: Recognized national/state parties get reserved party symbols. Independent candidates choose from a prescribed free symbols pool.
05

Campaign Period

Political parties engage voters through rallies, manifestos, and media under Model Code of Conduct monitoring.

Key Procedures: Campaigning stops 48 hours before the conclusion of poll (the Silence Period). Expenditure monitors track candidate spending.
06

Polling & EVM Voting

Voters cast secret ballots using EVMs with VVPAT audit slips at designated polling stations under security supervision.

Key Procedures: Mock poll verified by polling agents before voting starts. Secret voting compartment ensures ballot secrecy.
07

VVPAT Audit & Counting

Sealed Control Units and VVPAT drop boxes are brought to counting centers under Armed Guard protection for tallying.

Key Procedures: Electronic vote counts on Control Units are recorded round-by-round. Mandatory physical VVPAT slip counting in randomly selected polling stations verifies EVM accuracy.
08

Declaration of Results

The Returning Officer certifies candidate vote totals and formally declares the elected representative.

Key Procedures: Certificate of Election (Form 22) issued to winning candidate. Final result tally published in the Official Gazette.

Step-by-Step Polling Station Guide

Understand what happens when you step inside a polling station on election day.

1

First Polling Officer (Identification Check)

Checks your name on the electoral roll list and verifies your official photo identity document (Voter ID card / EPIC or approved ID proof).

2

Second Polling Officer (Indelible Ink & Register)

Applies indelible purple ink mark on your left forefinger, records your entry in Form 17A register, and issues your voter slip.

3

Third Polling Officer (Control Unit Release)

Takes your voter slip, presses the "BALLOT" button on the EVM Control Unit to unlock the Ballot Unit inside the voting booth, and directs you inside.

4

Voting Compartment (Cast Vote & VVPAT Check)

Inside the secret compartment, press the BLUE button next to your chosen candidate. Verify your choice on the 7-second VVPAT glass screen and listen for the 2-second BEEP confirmation tone.
